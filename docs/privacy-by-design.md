# Privacy by design

Case records may include special-category data (health, ethnicity, immigration, religion). The platform must:

1. **Minimise**: only fields a charity genuinely needs.
2. **Restrict**: role-based access plus per-case visibility levels.
3. **Log**: immutable audit trail of reads and writes of personal data.
4. **Protect**: encryption in transit and at rest; field-level encryption for sensitive notes.
5. **Retain**: configurable retention periods with automated review/deletion.
6. **Respect rights**: subject access, rectification, erasure and export tooling.
7. **Assess**: publish a DPIA template and keep one for the reference deployment.
8. **Safeguard**: support safeguarding flags and escalation workflows.

For UK deployments this aligns with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This document is not legal advice; deployers should take their own advice.
